# How to buy a bank auction property (step by step)

1. **Find a property** on Bidwallah and note the property ID, bank, branch, reserve price,
   EMD amount, EMD last date and auction date.
2. **Read the official sale notice** of the bank carefully (terms, dues, inspection date,
   e-auction portal, bid increment amount).
3. **Inspect the property** on the inspection date mentioned by the bank. Check physical
   condition, occupancy and surroundings.
4. **Do due diligence**: title documents, pending society / municipal / electricity dues,
   any court cases, and whether possession is physical or symbolic. Consulting a lawyer is recommended.
5. **Register on the e-auction portal** named in the notice and complete KYC
   (PAN, Aadhaar / address proof, photo). Get a digital signature certificate (DSC) if the portal requires it.
6. **Pay the EMD** (Earnest Money Deposit) before the EMD last date, usually by NEFT/RTGS to the
   account in the notice, and upload the bid form / documents on the portal.
7. **Take part in the online auction** on the auction date. Bids must be above the reserve price
   and increase by the bid increment amount. Auctions often auto-extend if a bid is placed in the last minutes.
8. **If you win**: the bank issues a sale confirmation. Typically 25% of the bid (including EMD) is paid
   immediately or by the next working day, and the balance within 15 days (the bank may extend this in
   writing, generally up to 3 months). Exact terms are in the sale notice.
9. **Sale certificate**: after full payment the bank issues a sale certificate. Register it at the
   sub-registrar office (stamp duty and registration charges are paid by the buyer).
10. **Possession**: the bank hands over possession (if physical possession). Then transfer utilities,
    society membership and mutation in municipal records to your name.

If you do not win, the EMD is refunded to your account, normally within a few working days.
If the winner fails to pay the balance on time, the EMD / amount paid can be forfeited.
