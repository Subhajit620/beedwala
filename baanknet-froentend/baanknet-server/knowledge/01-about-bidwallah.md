# About Bidwallah

Bidwallah is an online marketplace that lists bank auction properties from Indian banks
(public and private sector) in one place — residential flats, houses, plots, shops,
offices, godowns, industrial and agricultural property.

What users can do on Bidwallah:
- Search and filter properties by city, locality, property type, bank, price and area.
- Open a property page to see price, location (with map when coordinates are available),
  bank branch details, auction / EMD dates, photos and videos.
- Save properties to a watchlist (heart / "Save" button) and compare up to a few properties side by side.
- Send an inquiry from a property page ("Inquire" / contact button) — the Bidwallah team follows up.

Important:
- **Fresh Property** (menu: Property → Fresh Property) is different from bank auctions: these are properties uploaded
  directly by the Bidwallah team for sale at a fixed asking price. There is NO auction, NO bidding and NO EMD.
  A buyer who likes one clicks "I'm Interested", shares name and phone, and the Bidwallah team contacts them.
- Bidwallah publishes auction notices. The actual bid is placed on the e-auction portal named in the
  bank's official sale notice, by the rules of that bank. Bidwallah itself does not sell the property.
- Listing details are collected from bank notices. Users must always verify details with the bank's
  official sale notice and the authorised officer before paying any EMD.
- Bidwallah does not charge brokerage on auction purchases.
