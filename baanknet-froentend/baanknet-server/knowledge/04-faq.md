# Frequently asked questions

**Are bank auction properties cheaper?**
Often they are priced below market value, but not always. Compare with nearby market prices and add
costs: stamp duty, registration, pending dues, repairs and possible delay in possession.

**Can I get a home loan for an auction property?**
Yes, many banks give loans for auction properties, but the EMD and the first 25% payment usually
must come from your own funds because the time limit is short. Arrange loan approval in advance.

**Who can bid? Can NRIs bid?**
Any individual or entity eligible under the sale notice with valid KYC. NRIs can usually buy residential
or commercial property (not agricultural land / plantation / farmhouse) subject to FEMA rules.

**Can I inspect before bidding?**
Yes — the sale notice gives inspection dates and the authorised officer's contact. Always inspect.

**What if the auction is cancelled or postponed?**
Banks can cancel or postpone (for example if the borrower repays). Your EMD is then refunded.

**Is the price shown the final price?**
No. The listed price is normally the reserve price. The final price is the winning bid.

**How do I contact the bank?**
The property page shows the bank, branch / department and address when available. The official sale
notice contains the authorised officer's phone number and email.

**How do I contact Bidwallah?**
Use the "Inquire" button on any property page or the Contact page of the website.

**Is my EMD safe?**
Pay EMD only to the bank account mentioned in the official sale notice / e-auction portal — never to an
individual or agent. Bidwallah never asks users to pay EMD to Bidwallah.
